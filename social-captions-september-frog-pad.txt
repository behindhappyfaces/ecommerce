EVENT 2 — BUILD YOUR OWN FIRE CIDER (Wed, Sept 24, 6–8 PM)
------------------------------------------------------------
Cold and flu season is coming. Get ahead of it. 🔥

Join us at The Frog Pad to build your own fire cider — a traditional
apple cider vinegar tonic infused with horseradish, garlic, ginger,
citrus, and warming spices, used for generations to support the
immune system through the colder months.

Bottle your own jar to steep and take home, plus enjoy a warm
seasonal tasting and conversation on natural wellness as we head
into fall. Recipe card included, so you can make it again all season.

📍 The Frog Pad, Lakeway
