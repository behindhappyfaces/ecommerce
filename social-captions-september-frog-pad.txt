EVENT 1 — BAKE FOR A BIGGER PURPOSE (Wed, Sept 10, 6–8 PM)
------------------------------------------------------------
Bake for a bigger purpose. 🍞

With Rosh Hashanah beginning September 11, join us at The Frog Pad
for a hands-on challah bread making workshop. You'll learn to shape

Come bake. Come gather. Give back.

EVENT 2 — BUILD YOUR OWN FIRE CIDER (Wed, Sept 24, 6–8 PM)
------------------------------------------------------------
Take a little time for yourself. 🔥

Join us at The Frog Pad to build your own fire cider — a traditional
apple cider vinegar tonic infused with horseradish, garlic, ginger,
citrus, and warming spices, used for generations to support a
healthy immune system. Bottle your own jar to steep and take home,
recipe card included.

With National Women's Health & Fitness Day taking place in
September, this one's for the whole woman: health, connection, and
well-being. A portion of proceeds benefits Hill Country Women's
Health Collective.

📍 The Frog Pad, Lakeway
🗓️ Wednesday, September 24 | 6:00–8:00 PM
🎟️ Ticket price TBD — link in bio for details and to reserve.
